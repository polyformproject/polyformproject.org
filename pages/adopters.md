title: Adopters
---

As of November, 2026, PolyForm project no longer publishes a list of adopters.

Sincerest thanks to all the projects that have adopted PolyForm terms, especially those that did so early on in the project's history, and double so those that reached out to add them the adopters page.

There are now too many projects to showcase in one place, especially curating manually.  For a sense of what's out there, try [searching for `polyformproject.org` in license files on GitHub](https://github.com/search?q=polyformproject.org+AND+path%3Alicense&type=code) or another forge.
